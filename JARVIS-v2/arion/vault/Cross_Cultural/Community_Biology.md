# Community Biology & Nature

- Regular communal gathering → **lower cortisol**, higher **oxytocin**
- Reduces loneliness (WHO: loneliness kills as much as smoking 15 cigarettes/day)
- **Blue Zones** (longest-living communities globally) — all have strong community/faith practices

## Nature Connection
- **Forest bathing (Shinrin-yoku)** — proven to lower blood pressure, boost NK cells (cancer fighters)
- All cultures had sacred groves, rivers, mountains — protected ecology through religion
