# Meditation Research

- **Harvard study**: 8 weeks of meditation shrinks amygdala (fear center)
- Increases **gray matter density** in prefrontal cortex
- Lengthens **telomeres** (cellular age markers)
- Activates **default mode network** reduction = less mind-wandering = more presence
