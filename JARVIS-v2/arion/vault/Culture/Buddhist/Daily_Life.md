# Buddhist Daily Life Patterns

- **Meditation** — 1-3 hours daily for monks, growing for laypeople
- **Mindful eating** — eat slowly, chewing fully, gratitude
- **Dana** (generosity) — give without expectation
- **Ahimsa** — non-harm to all sentient beings
- **Noble Silence** — periods of no speech for inner clarity
- **Prostrations** — physical humility practice
- **Walking meditation** (Kinhin) — mindfulness in movement
