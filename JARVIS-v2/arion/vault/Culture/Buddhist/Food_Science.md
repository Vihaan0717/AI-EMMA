| Practice | Scientific Backing |
|---|---|

| Mindful eating | Reduces overeating, improves digestion |
| Eating before noon (monks) | Intermittent fasting benefits |
| Tea ceremony (Zen) | Catechins in green tea, calm alertness |
| Simple diet | Reduces decision fatigue, metabolic clarity |
