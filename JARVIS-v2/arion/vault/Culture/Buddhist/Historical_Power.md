# Historical Power & Modern Damage

## How Early Buddhism Was Powerful
- **Nalanda University** (5th century CE) — world's first residential university, 10,000 students, 2,000 teachers, 9 million books
- Spread across Asia without violence — purely through philosophical power
- **Buddhist logic** (Dignaga, Dharmakirti) — advanced epistemology
- **Meditation science** now validated by modern neuroscience (Harvard, MIT research)
- **Psychology of mind** — mapped thousands of mental states before Western psychology existed
- **Ashoka the Great** — converted after war, spread Buddhism, built hospitals, roads, wells

## How Modern Culture Damages Buddhist Culture
- **McMindfulness** — stripped of ethics, sold as productivity tool
- Commercialization of meditation apps without depth
- Tibetan Buddhism under political persecution
- Young Asians ashamed of "old religion"
- Environmental destruction contradicting Buddhist interdependence values
- Instagram Buddhism — aesthetic without practice
