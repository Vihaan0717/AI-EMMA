# Buddhist Philosophy & Values

- **Four Noble Truths**:
  1. Life contains suffering (Dukkha)
  2. Suffering has a cause (craving/attachment)
  3. Suffering can end (Nirvana)
  4. Path to end suffering (Noble Eightfold Path)
- **Noble Eightfold Path**: Right View, Intention, Speech, Action, Livelihood, Effort, Mindfulness, Concentration
- **Anicca** — impermanence of all things
- **Anatta** — no permanent self (ego is illusion)
- **Interdependence** — all phenomena arise in relation to each other

## Values System
- **Compassion (Karuna)** for all beings
- **Loving-kindness (Metta)** — active goodwill
- **Equanimity (Upekkha)** — non-attached mind
- **Beginner's Mind** — open to all possibilities
- **Interdependence** → environmental ethics built-in

## Sacred Texts
- **Tripitaka (Pali Canon)** — oldest Buddhist scriptures
- **Dhammapada** — practical wisdom verses
- **Heart Sutra** — emptiness and form
- **Lotus Sutra** — universal Buddha-nature
- **Tibetan Book of the Dead (Bardo Thodol)** — consciousness at death
