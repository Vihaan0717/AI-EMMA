# Christian Daily Life Patterns

- **Morning prayer/devotion** (reading Bible, meditation)
- **Sunday worship** — communal gathering, sermon, communion
- **Grace before meals** — gratitude practice
- **Confession** (Catholic) — psychological release of guilt
- **Sabbath rest** (Sunday/Saturday) — sacred rest, prevents burnout
- **Christmas** (birth), **Easter** (resurrection) as central festivals
- **Charity culture** — strong emphasis; hospitals, schools, orphanages built globally
