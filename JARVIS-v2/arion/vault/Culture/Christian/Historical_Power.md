# Historical Power & Modern Damage

## How Early Christianity Was Powerful
- Built world's first **hospitals** (4th century, Byzantine Empire)
- **Universities** founded by Church (Bologna 1088, Oxford 1096, etc.)
- **Preservation of knowledge** through monasteries during Dark Ages
- **Music**: Gregorian chant, Bach, classical tradition rooted in worship
- **Art**: Sistine Chapel, cathedrals — embodying spiritual vision in matter
- **Social revolution**: Abolished practices of leaving sick/elderly to die
- **Printing press** spread primarily for Bible → democratized knowledge

## How Modern Culture Damages Christian Culture
- Secularism removing Sabbath rest → burnout epidemic
- Consumerist Christmas → losing spiritual meaning
- Declining community → isolation epidemic
- Church scandals destroying trust in institution
- **Prosperity Gospel** — distorting core message of sacrifice/love
- Young people losing connection to deep theological tradition
