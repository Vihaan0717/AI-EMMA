# Christian Philosophy & Values

- **Trinity** — Father, Son (Jesus Christ), Holy Spirit as one God
- **Grace** — salvation as gift, not earned
- **Agape** — unconditional divine love
- **Redemption** — everyone can be forgiven and renewed
- **Stewardship** — humans as caretakers of Earth
- **Imago Dei** — humans made in image of God → inherent dignity

## Values System
- **Love thy neighbor** — radical inclusion
- **Forgiveness** — releasing resentment (psychologically proven beneficial)
- **Humility** — servant leadership model
- **Hope** — future-orientation in suffering
- **Community/Church** — belonging as spiritual need

## Sacred Texts
- **Bible** (Old + New Testament)
- **Old Testament** — shared with Jewish tradition
- **New Testament** — Gospels, Acts, Letters, Revelation
- **Psalms** — emotional prayer poetry (used in therapy)
- **Proverbs** — practical wisdom
