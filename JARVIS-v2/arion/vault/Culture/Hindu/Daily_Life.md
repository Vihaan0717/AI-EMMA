# Hindu Daily Life Patterns

- **Brahma Muhurta** — wake 1.5 hrs before sunrise (4-6 AM), considered sacred for learning
- **Morning ritual**: bath, prayer (puja), lighting incense/lamp (diya)
- **Sandhyavandanam** — three daily prayer times (sunrise, noon, sunset)
- **Namaste** — greeting acknowledging divine in other
- **Meals**: often vegetarian, eaten on floor (grounding), right hand used
- **Tulsi plant** in every home — considered sacred, has antibacterial properties
- **Elders touched at feet** — respect for wisdom and accumulated karma
