|---|---|

| Eating on banana leaf | Natural antimicrobial compounds |
| Neem use (Datun) | Antibacterial, antifungal |
| Ghee consumption | Butyric acid — gut health, brain fuel |
| Yoga | Nervous system regulation, vagus nerve stimulation |
| Pranayama (breathing) | CO2 tolerance, parasympathetic activation |
