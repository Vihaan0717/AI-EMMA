# Historical Power & Modern Damage

## How Early Hinduism Was Powerful
- **Mathematics**: Zero invented, decimal system, algebra (Aryabhata, Brahmagupta)
- **Surgery**: Sushruta Samhita — first documented surgical procedures (rhinoplasty, cataracts)
- **Astronomy**: Accurate calculation of Earth's circumference, heliocentric concepts
- **Metallurgy**: Delhi Iron Pillar (1600 years old, no rust — still unexplained fully)
- **Architecture**: Temples built with seismic resistance, water harvesting systems
- **Ayurveda**: Complete medical system — 300+ surgical tools documented

## How Modern Culture Damages Hindu Culture
- Consumerism replacing ritual meaning
- Fast food replacing Ayurvedic diet knowledge
- Nuclear families destroying joint family wisdom transfer
- Dismissal of Sanskrit as "dead language" (losing encoded knowledge)
- Commercialization of festivals (Diwali → crackers pollution, not inner light)
- Yoga separated from philosophy (became only physical exercise)
