# Hindu Philosophy

- **Dharma** — righteous duty/cosmic order
- **Karma** — action and its consequences across lifetimes
- **Moksha** — liberation from cycle of birth/death (samsara)
- **Ahimsa** — non-violence (influenced Gandhi, influenced world)
- **Brahman** — ultimate reality, all is one consciousness
- **Atman** — individual soul, identical to Brahman

## Values System
- **Satyam** (Truth), **Shivam** (Auspiciousness), **Sundaram** (Beauty)
- Family as sacred unit
- Guru-Shishya (teacher-student) lineage system
- **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam** — "The whole world is one family"

## Sacred Texts
- **Vedas** (4) — oldest known scriptures (~1500-1200 BCE)
- **Upanishads** — philosophical core of Hinduism
- **Bhagavad Gita** — duty, action, devotion (battlefield wisdom)
- **Ramayana** — values of dharma, loyalty, courage
- **Mahabharata** — complexity of moral choices
- **Puranas** — cosmology, mythology, history
