# Muslim Daily Life Patterns

- **Fajr** prayer before sunrise, **Dhuhr** noon, **Asr** afternoon, **Maghrib** sunset, **Isha** night
- **Prayer** = 5x daily mindfulness practice (proven stress reducer)
- **Wudu** (ritual washing before prayer) — 5x daily
- **Bismillah** said before any action (eating, working, etc.)
- **Friday Jumu'ah** — communal prayer, community bonding
- **Halal** dietary laws — specific food standards
- **Hospitality** — strong emphasis; guests honored as sacred duty
- **Ramadan** — 29-30 days of fasting, deep community bonding
