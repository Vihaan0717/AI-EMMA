# Historical Power & Modern Damage

## How Early Islam Was Powerful
- **Golden Age (8th-13th century)**: Baghdad as world's knowledge capital
- **Al-Khwarizmi**: Invented Algebra (Algorithm = his name)
- **Ibn Sina (Avicenna)**: Canon of Medicine — used in Europe for 600 years
- **Al-Biruni**: Calculated Earth's circumference with 1% accuracy
- **Ibn al-Haytham**: Father of Optics — first scientific method
- **Al-Zahrawi**: Father of Surgery — 200+ surgical instruments
- **Rumi, Hafiz**: Poetry exploring consciousness, love, divine
- **House of Wisdom** (Bayt al-Hikmah) — preserved Greek knowledge + added Islamic knowledge

## How Modern Culture Damages Muslim Culture
- Extremism misrepresenting the peace-centered faith
- Western media creating monolithic negative image
- Young Muslims losing Arabic — losing direct Quranic access
- Materialism replacing zakat spirit
- Political Islam replacing spiritual Islam
- Loss of Islamic philosophy tradition (Sufism marginalized)
