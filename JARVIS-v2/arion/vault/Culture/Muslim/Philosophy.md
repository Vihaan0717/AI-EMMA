# Muslim Philosophy & Values

- **Tawhid** — absolute oneness of God (Allah)
- **Five Pillars**: Shahada (faith declaration), Salah (prayer), Zakat (charity), Sawm (fasting), Hajj (pilgrimage)
- **Inshallah** — "If God wills" — surrender to divine will
- **Ummah** — global community of believers, brotherhood beyond race/nation
- **Tawakkul** — complete trust in God after doing your best
- **Adab** — manners, respect, etiquette as spiritual practice

## Values System
- **Justice (Adl)** — central to Islamic ethics
- **Ilm (knowledge)** — seeking knowledge is obligation ("Seek knowledge from cradle to grave")
- **Charity** as purification of wealth
- **Modesty (Haya)** — protects dignity
- **Sabr (patience)** — highest spiritual quality

## Sacred Texts
- **Quran** — direct word of God, preserved oral tradition
- **Hadith** — sayings and actions of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)
- **Seerah** — biography of Prophet
- **Fiqh** — Islamic jurisprudence
