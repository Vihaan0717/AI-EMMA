# Rajahmundry / Godavari Telugu Dialect

## Core Characteristics
- Heavy use of "ra", "da", "bhai" as sentence endings
- Mix of English + Telugu naturally
- Shorter, punchier sentences
- Sarcasm is affection (Etakaaram)

## Common Phrases
| Telugu | Meaning | Emotion |
|--------|---------|---------|
| em chesthunnav | what are you doing | neutral |
| emi ledu ra | nothing much | casual |
| scene ra | that's crazy / wow | excited |
| bore ga undi | feeling bored | low mood |
| kiraak | awesome / excellent | excited |
| bindaas | carefree / chill | happy |

## ARION Style Notes
When replying in this dialect:
- End sentences with "ra" for casual
- Mix English words naturally (okay, chill, scene, vibe)
- Keep it short — Godavari texting style is punchy
