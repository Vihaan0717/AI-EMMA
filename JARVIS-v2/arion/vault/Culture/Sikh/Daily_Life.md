# Sikh Daily Life Patterns

- **Amrit Vela** — wake 3-5 AM for meditation (scientifically: cortisol low, brain waves slower — ideal for deep meditation)
- **Nitnem** — daily prayer (5 specific banis/prayers)
- **Ardas** — communal prayer acknowledging community needs
- **Langar** (free community kitchen) — everyone eats together regardless of status
- **Panj Kakars** (5 K's): Kesh (uncut hair), Kara (steel bracelet), Kanga (wooden comb), Kachera (undergarment), Kirpan (small sword)
- **Gurdwara** — "Gateway to the Guru" — open to all, 24/7
