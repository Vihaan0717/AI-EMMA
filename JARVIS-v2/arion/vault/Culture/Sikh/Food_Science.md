# Sikh Food Culture & Science

| Practice | Scientific Backing |
|---|---|
| Langar (community meals) | Social eating → mental health, loneliness prevention |
| Vegetarian langar traditionally | Reduces environmental impact, health benefits |
| No beef/pork in langar | Inclusive of Hindu and Muslim visitors |
| Sarso da saag + makki di roti | Mustard greens rich in Vitamin K, bone health |
| Lassi (buttermilk) | Probiotics, gut microbiome health |
| Early morning meditation | Alpha/theta brainwave states, cortisol management |
