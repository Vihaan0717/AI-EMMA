# Historical Power & Modern Damage

## How Early Sikhism Was Powerful
- Founded during Mughal era (1469 CE) — stood against religious oppression
- **Guru Nanak Dev Ji** traveled 28,000 km across 4 directions spreading message of equality
- Sikh armies protected non-Sikh communities from persecution
- **Banda Singh Bahadur** — abolished feudal zamindari system, gave land to peasants (1710 CE)
- **Gurbani (hymns)** — evidence of musical therapy (ragas matched to times of day)
- Sikh warriors defeated Afghan invaders who had defeated everyone else

## How Modern Culture Damages Sikh Culture
- Youth losing Punjabi → losing access to Gurbani directly
- Langar becoming ceremony not daily practice
- **Khalistan political movements** distorting spiritual message
- Western youth disconnecting from Amrit Vela discipline
- Hair (Kesh) seen as burden rather than identity/blessing
- Materialism replacing seva culture
