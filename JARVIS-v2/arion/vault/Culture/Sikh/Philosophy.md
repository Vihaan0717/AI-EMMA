# Sikh Philosophy & Values

- **Ik Onkar** — One God, creator of all
- **Waheguru** — name of God, meaning "Wonderful Teacher"
- **Three Pillars**: Nam Japna (meditate on God's name), Kirat Karo (honest living), Vand Chhako (share with others)
- **Seva** — selfless service as highest spiritual practice
- **Sarbat da Bhala** — welfare of all humanity
- **No caste system** — radical equality from founding

## Values System
- **Chardi Kala** — eternal optimism, rising spirit even in suffering
- **Nimrata** — humility
- **Courage** — stand against injustice (defend the defenseless)
- **Equality** — women have equal spiritual status from day 1
- **Truth** above all

## Sacred Texts
- **Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji** — living Guru, scripture of Sikhism
- Contains poetry of **36 composers** from different religions/castes
- Written in **Gurmukhi script** — created by Guru Angad Dev Ji
- **Japji Sahib** — opening prayer, cosmological worldview
