# Psychological Developmental Stages

## Erikson's 8 Stages of Life
| Stage | Age | Core Conflict | Healthy Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–18 months | Trust vs Mistrust | Hope |
| 2 | 2–3 yrs | Autonomy vs Shame | Will |
| 3 | 3–5 yrs | Initiative vs Guilt | Purpose |
| 4 | 6–11 yrs | Industry vs Inferiority | Competence |
| 5 | 12–18 yrs | Identity vs Role confusion | Fidelity |
| 6 | 19–40 yrs | Intimacy vs Isolation | Love |
| 7 | 40–65 yrs | Generativity vs Stagnation | Care |
| 8 | 65+ | Integrity vs Despair | Wisdom |

## Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs
```
        Self-Actualization (purpose, creativity)
            Esteem (respect, achievement)
          Love/Belonging (family, friends, community)
        Safety (security, health, order)
    Physiological (food, water, shelter, sleep)
```
