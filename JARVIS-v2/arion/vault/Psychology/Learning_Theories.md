# How Humans Learn

## Learning Theories
| Theory | Thinker | Core Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Classical Conditioning | Pavlov | Stimulus-response association |
| Operant Conditioning | Skinner | Behavior shaped by rewards/punishment |
| Constructivism | Piaget | Knowledge built from experience |
| Zone of Proximal Development | Vygotsky | Learn best just beyond current ability |
| Multiple Intelligences | Howard Gardner | 8 types of intelligence |
| Spaced Repetition | Ebbinghaus | Review at intervals to defeat forgetting curve |
| Deliberate Practice | Anders Ericsson | Focused, feedback-driven practice builds mastery |

## The Forgetting Curve (Ebbinghaus)
- Without review: forget **50%** in 1 hour, **70%** in 24 hours, **90%** in 1 week
- **Solution for Obsidian**: Use spaced repetition + linking notes

## Neuroplasticity
- Brain rewires itself with repeated experience
- **Hebb's Law**: "Neurons that fire together, wire together"
- Learning creates **new synaptic connections**
- Peak plasticity: childhood, but never truly stops
